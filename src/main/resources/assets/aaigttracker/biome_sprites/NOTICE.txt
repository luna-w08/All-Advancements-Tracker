Biome icon artwork

These 16x16 icons were generated specifically for All Advancements IGT Tracker
using OpenAI image generation and exported to a limited pixel-art palette.
They were not downloaded from Minecraft Wiki or extracted from Minecraft.

Generation prompts and the licence review are recorded in the project's
art/biome-sprite-prompts.json and docs/biome-artwork.md files.

Minecraft names and existing game content belong to their respective owners.
